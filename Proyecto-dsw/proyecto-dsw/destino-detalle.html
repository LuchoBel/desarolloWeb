Kyoto, Japan

Featured Best time: Oct-Nov · Currency: yen · Language: Japanese

Ideal 7 days · Mid-range budget €900-1200 incl. flight · JR Pass recommended

What to see

Fushimi Inari, Arashiyama, Kinkaku-ji, Gion and a day trip to Nara. Buy your eSIM before flying and book accommodation in Higashiyama.

Community tips

  • Arrive at Fushimi Inari early (7am) to avoid the crowds.
  • Use the bus + bike; the metro doesn't cover everything.
  • Store your luggage in station lockers.
